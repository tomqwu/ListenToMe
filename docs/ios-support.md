# Listen To Me — iPhone and iPad support

Contact **[tom@cloudnativity.io](mailto:tom@cloudnativity.io)** for help. Include your iOS version, device model, and the app version shown in Settings → About. Do not include API keys or private transcripts.

## Getting started

Requires iOS or iPadOS 26 or later. Tap Start listening and allow microphone access. Speech language assets may download on first use; your language choice is remembered across launches. With the language set to Automatic (the default), the app detects the language you speak on your device during the first seconds of each recording and, if it differs, switches and transcribes the recording again. It checks again about once a minute while people talk, so a recording that changes language part-way switches too, and only the part since the change is transcribed again ("Detected … from 2:05 · switched"); the first time, a language model of about 75 MB downloads from huggingface.co. Detection runs on this device, and no audio is sent anywhere for it. If you had chosen a language before version 1.12.0, it stays fixed; choose Automatic to turn detection on. Tap Undo next to "Detected … · switched" to go back to the language before the latest switch and keep it (earlier switches stay), or choose a fixed language in More → Settings → Transcription. A recording that continues the conversation, for example after a call, stays in the detected language. "Switched for new speech" means the audio before the switch was no longer held (the app keeps only the last two minutes), so the earlier lines were not transcribed again. Recording works while the app is open; it does not record other apps or phone calls. A call, Siri or an alarm pauses capture, the status line says why, and recording resumes when the interruption ends.

Use Notes to add context, Save to keep a conversation, and History to reopen, search or share it. Sharing offers readable text and Markdown. More → What's New shows the release version and recent changes, and appears after an update only when those notes actually changed.

## Speaker labels

Identify speakers is on unless you turn it off in More → Settings → Speakers (the switch is available while not recording). Identification runs on this device and uses more battery while listening. The speaker models download once from huggingface.co: when the app opens with the setting on, when you turn it on, or when you start a recording, and recording never waits for them. Settings → Speakers shows the download and says when the models are ready, or why they failed, with Retry. With the models ready, the status under the conversation title reads "Speaker labels on". While the models download, or when labels are unavailable, a speaker line appears below it ("preparing models…", or "unavailable" with Retry). Record as usual: lines start as Microphone and change to Speaker 1, Speaker 2… after the first pass, about 20 seconds into a recording, and the last lines are labeled when you stop, so a short recording is labeled at Stop once the models are ready. To rename a speaker, open Notes → Speakers, type the name and press Done; the new name appears on every line, in History search and in shared text and Markdown. Summaries pick up the new name the next time they are generated. Labels are estimates: overlapping speech and very short phrases often keep Microphone. Within one recording, someone who stays quiet for a while keeps their label and name when they speak again, though two very similar voices can occasionally be mixed up or split. A voice is not recognized again after you stop and start listening. Status details (under Quick Summary) lists whether Identify speakers is on, whether the models are ready, how many passes ran and the last speaker error.

## AI summaries

A new install summarizes on-device with Apple Intelligence where the device supports it. To use Ollama instead, enter your own Ollama Cloud API key in Settings, or the address of an Ollama server you run on your own network — use the computer's `.local` name, for example `http://your-mac.local:11434`, because a plain-`http` numeric address such as `192.168.1.10` is refused. Ollama account limits apply and internet access is required for cloud features. The app is free; third-party service access may have its own costs.

Auto reacts to new speech. Quick provides short key points; Summary and Deep update when the conversation merits a fuller review. They can also be generated manually. When Auto appears inactive, open Status details and check the provider and model status. A brief pause to combine incoming speech is normal; unchanged input does not require a new summary.

## Common fixes

- No transcript: check microphone permission, selected language and downloaded speech assets.
- Transcript in the wrong language: Automatic checks the first stretch of speech, then again about once a minute while people talk. A later change of language must be heard twice in a row (about 20 seconds apart) before it switches, so a short phrase in another language does not switch, and a switch stays for at least 90 seconds; a recording switches at most four times. A change is noticed one to two minutes after it happens. Re-checks pause in Low Power Mode, while the app is in the background, while the device is hot and during silence. Detection needs about 8 seconds of speech and is skipped when it is not confident, and it cannot switch to a language your device has no speech model for. Words from two languages mixed within one sentence are not supported: the transcriber hears one language at a time. Choose the language in More → Settings → Transcription to fix it for every recording.
- "Language detection paused": the language model could not be downloaded or loaded, for example without internet on first use. Recording continues in the starting language; the next recording tries again.
- Cloud error: test the connection in Settings, check your key and account limits, and refresh the available model list. For your own server, check the `.local` address and allow local network access.
- Apple Intelligence unavailable: check device support and enable Apple Intelligence in system settings, or select Ollama.
- Recording stopped: backgrounding stops recording and saves the conversation; a call, Siri or an alarm pauses it and it resumes on its own.
- No speaker labels yet: read the status under the conversation title. "Preparing models" means the one-time download is still running; a recording that ends before it finishes keeps Microphone, because Stop never starts or waits for the download. Once it says "Speaker labels on", even a short recording is labeled when you stop.
- "Speaker labels unavailable": the speaker models could not be loaded, for example without internet on first use. Check your connection and tap Retry next to the line or in Settings → Speakers; starting a new recording also tries again. Recording and transcription keep working meanwhile.
- Missing from a restore: check whether "Exclude conversations from iCloud backup" is on in Settings.
