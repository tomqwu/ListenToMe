Listen To Me captures conversations with live on-device microphone transcription, editable notes and local history on iPhone and iPad. Follow the compact transcript alongside Quick Summary, which can evaluate new live and completed speech and update only when information changes. With Auto enabled, meaningful context also triggers Summary and substantive questions or tradeoffs trigger Deep. Manual Generate remains available. Each output has its own model selection and readable formatting.

Use optional Apple Intelligence summaries on supported devices, or enter your own Ollama Cloud API key in Settings. The key is stored in this device's Keychain. Refresh models from the API and test the connection. Notes and transcripts are sent to Ollama when you request a summary or enable Auto summaries; microphone audio stays on-device.

Add local photos and files, import context from Calendar, or send compatible copies from Apple Notes through the share extension. Original attachments stay local; only text added to Notes is included in summaries. Save, reopen, delete and share conversations in readable text or Markdown.

Requires iOS or iPadOS 26 or later and a supported speech language. Live transcription requires a physical device. Recording works in the foreground and does not capture other apps or phone calls. Please test recording, summaries, imports and the refreshed light/dark design, and report problems through TestFlight feedback.

See the installed version and recent changes in What’s New after an update, or reopen the changelog from the More menu.
