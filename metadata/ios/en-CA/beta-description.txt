Listen To Me captures conversations with live on-device microphone transcription, editable notes and local history on iPhone and iPad. Follow the compact transcript alongside Quick Summary, which can evaluate new live and completed speech and update only when information changes. With Auto enabled, meaningful context also triggers Summary; Deep analysis is on the Mac app only. Manual Generate remains available, suggested prompts under Quick Summary answer a question with one tap, and Summarize selection summarizes just the lines you pick. Each output has its own model selection and readable formatting. Rotate iPhone for side-by-side Transcript and Quick. Prompt below Notes supports your own one-time questions or saved instructions for this conversation, with optional editable domain presets.

Summaries run on-device with Apple Intelligence by default on supported devices. To use Ollama instead, enter your own Ollama Cloud API key in Settings, or the address of an Ollama server you run on your own network. The key is stored in this device's Keychain. Refresh models from the server and test the connection. Notes and transcripts are sent to the selected Ollama server when you request a summary or enable Auto summaries; microphone audio stays on-device. You can also choose an OpenAI-compatible server you enter yourself, such as LM Studio on your Mac or OpenRouter; Settings says "your server" or "may leave this device". Or choose a Cloud AI service — OpenAI, Claude, Gemini, DeepSeek, OpenRouter, Groq, Mistral or xAI Grok — with your own API key; Settings says "Sends your transcript to" that vendor.

Add local photos and files, import context from Calendar, or send compatible copies from Apple Notes through the share extension. Original attachments stay local; only text added to Notes is included in summaries. Conversations name themselves after you stop. Save, reopen, delete and share conversations, export them as Markdown, plain text, SRT or WebVTT subtitles or JSON, search History by meaning and ask your history a question. Vocabularies include English and Simplified Chinese terms across seven domains; My terms helps recognize your own names and jargon, and you can optionally keep each recording's audio on this device to play it back. Optionally, Save to folder writes each conversation as a Markdown note into a folder you pick, such as an Obsidian vault or iCloud Drive.

Requires iOS or iPadOS 26 or later and a supported speech language. Live transcription requires a physical device. Recording works in the foreground and does not capture other apps or phone calls. Please test recording, summaries, imports and the refreshed light/dark design, and report problems through TestFlight feedback.
