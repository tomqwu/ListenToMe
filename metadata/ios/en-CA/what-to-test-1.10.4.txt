What's New now appears only when the bundled notes actually change, and a fresh install no longer sees "in this update". Shared imports that fail are set aside and deleted after 24 hours instead of blocking later imports. Your transcription language choice is remembered across launches. Conversation and attachment files are written with device data protection, and Settings has a new toggle to keep conversations, attachments and shared imports out of iCloud and Finder backups. History has a search field. On an Apple Intelligence install the app never contacts ollama.com; the model list refreshes only after you choose Ollama and add a key or your own server.
