This build turns speaker labels on by default and adds automatic language detection. Both run on this device: your audio is not saved or sent anywhere. Two models download once from huggingface.co: the speaker models when the app opens, and the language model (about 75 MB) the first time a recording hears speech. Later recordings use them without the network.

Please test:
1. After updating, open More → Settings → Speakers. Identify speakers is on (unless you had turned it off), and the models download, then show as ready, without recording.
2. With two people talking, record for about 30 seconds and press Stop. Lines are labeled Speaker 1 and Speaker 2. Open Notes → Speakers, give each speaker a name and press Done, then check the names appear on their lines.
3. Open More → Settings → Transcription → Language and check it says Automatic (default). If you had picked a language before, it stays picked; choose Automatic to test.
4. Tap New and speak a language other than your phone's for at least 30 seconds. Within about 30 seconds the status reads "Detected … · switched" and the earlier lines are written again in that language. Keep talking during the switch and check no words are missing.
5. Tap Undo on that notice. The transcript returns to your phone's language and Settings shows that language picked. Set it back to Automatic afterwards.
6. Speak your phone's language with Automatic on: nothing switches.
7. Optional: with Airplane mode on before the language model has ever downloaded, recording still works and the status says language detection is paused.
