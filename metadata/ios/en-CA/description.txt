Turn conversations into notes you can return to.

Listen To Me brings live microphone transcription, editable notes and conversation summaries to iPhone and iPad. Follow the transcript as you speak, capture your own thoughts alongside it, and keep everything together in local conversation history.

CAPTURE AND ORGANIZE
• Transcribe speech on-device with Apple's SpeechAnalyzer.
• Optionally repair likely misheard words with a fast Flash model. Compare or restore each original.
• See transcript, Quick Summary and Deep Summary together.
• Add notes, photos and files, and give each conversation a title.
• Import compatible copies from Apple Notes through the share extension, or add context from a selected calendar event.
• Save conversations and reopen them from History. Search past conversations, swipe right to share, left to delete, or touch and hold for actions.
• Share transcripts, notes and summaries as readable text or Markdown.

CHOOSE HOW TO SUMMARIZE
Choose a full Summary, a brief Quick Summary or a Deep Think analysis of decisions, risks and next steps. Each output is saved separately. Enable Auto Quick Summary for live updates while listening when the text changes, with a 15-second minimum interval between automatic requests. A visible status shows when Auto is off, waiting, updating or retrying. Apple Intelligence on a supported device is the default, or choose Ollama — Ollama Cloud with your own API key, or an Ollama server you run on your own network. Refresh the model list directly from Ollama and select a model for each mode from available models, including DeepSeek, GLM, Qwen and Kimi. Available models and variants depend on Ollama's current catalog.

YOU CONTROL CLOUD USE
Microphone speech is transcribed on-device; raw audio is not saved. Conversations are stored locally with device data protection, and a setting keeps them out of iCloud and Finder backups. When you choose Ollama and generate an AI output or enable Auto Quick Summary, your notes and transcript are sent to the server shown in Settings: Ollama Cloud, or the server you entered yourself. Enabling AI speech correction separately sends new completed phrases and nearby transcript text to an independently selected Flash model. Original wording is kept for review and restore. Your API key is stored in this device's Keychain. An imported calendar event has join-link passcodes and e-mail addresses removed before it reaches your notes. The full privacy policy and support contact are linked in Settings.

Requires iOS or iPadOS 26 or later. Speech transcription depends on device and language support, with language assets downloaded when needed. Apple Intelligence summaries require a supported device and enabled Apple Intelligence; where it is unavailable the app falls back to Ollama and says why. Ollama Cloud requires an internet connection and your own account access. Recording uses the microphone while the app is in the foreground; it does not capture other apps or phone calls, and it resumes on its own after a call, Siri or an alarm. Review AI-generated summaries and transcript corrections for accuracy.
