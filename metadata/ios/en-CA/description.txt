Turn conversations into notes you can return to — privately, on your iPhone and iPad.

Listen To Me transcribes conversations live on your device, labels who is speaking, and turns what was said into summaries, answers and searchable notes. Everything stays on your device unless you choose otherwise.

TRANSCRIBE ANYTHING
• Live, on-device transcription with Apple's speech engine.
• Speaker names appear automatically. Rename anyone, and they keep their name even after a long pause.
• Automatic language detection: if the conversation switches language partway through, the app follows it and rewrites the earlier lines. You can also pick from every language your device supports.
• Vocabularies for Medical, Legal, Software & Cloud, Engineering, Finance, Sales and Education & Research, plus your own My terms list for names and jargon in any language, help get specialist words right. The app suggests the right vocabulary when a conversation sounds like one of these fields.
• Optional AI correction fixes likely misheard words, and you can review or restore the original.

SUMMARIZE AND ASK
• Quick Summary keeps up while you listen. Summary covers the whole conversation. Both handle long meetings and tell you what they used.
• Suggested questions appear under Quick. Tap one to get an answer.
• Select any lines and summarize just that part.
• Conversations name themselves after you stop.

FIND IT LATER
• History search understands meaning, not just exact words.
• Ask your history a question and get an answer that links to the conversations it came from.
• Add notes, photos, files and calendar context to any conversation.

SHARE AND KEEP
• Export as Markdown, plain text, SRT or WebVTT subtitles, or JSON.
• Save every conversation automatically as a Markdown note in a folder you choose, such as an Obsidian vault in iCloud Drive.
• Optionally keep the audio with each conversation and tap any line to hear that moment.
• Delete one conversation, or all of them, at any time.

YOUR AI, YOUR CHOICE
Apple Intelligence on your device is the default. If you prefer, connect your own:
• Ollama, on your own computer or Ollama Cloud.
• Any OpenAI-compatible server, such as LM Studio, vLLM or llama.cpp.
• A cloud AI service with your own API key: OpenAI, Anthropic Claude, Google Gemini, DeepSeek, OpenRouter, Groq, Mistral or xAI.
Each option says clearly where your text goes. You pick the model, and the app never chooses a paid model for you.

PRIVATE BY DEFAULT
Transcription, speaker labels, language detection, vocabularies and search run on your device. Audio is not kept unless you turn on Save audio recordings, and saved audio never leaves your device and is excluded from backups. Conversations are stored with device data protection, and you can keep them out of iCloud and Finder backups. Text is sent off your device only when you choose an outside AI service, and only to that service. API keys are stored in your device's Keychain. The full privacy policy and support contact are linked in Settings.

Requires iOS or iPadOS 26 or later. Speech recognition, speaker labels and language detection depend on device and language support; the models download once on first use. Apple Intelligence features require a supported device with Apple Intelligence turned on. Cloud services require an internet connection and your own account. Recording uses the microphone while the app is in the foreground; it does not record other apps or phone calls, and it resumes on its own after a call, Siri or an alarm. Review AI-generated summaries and corrections for accuracy.
