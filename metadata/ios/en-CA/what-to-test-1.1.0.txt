New in 1.1.0: Ollama Cloud summaries with your own API key and a live model catalog.

1. Open Settings → Summary provider → Ollama Cloud. Enter your own key and tap Save API key. Close and reopen the app to check that the saved-key indicator persists.
2. Tap Refresh models from API, then Choose model. Check the available DeepSeek, GLM, Qwen and Kimi options. Flash and Pro variants appear only when Ollama returns them; no model download to your phone is needed.
3. Tap Test connection. This sends a short test prompt, without your conversation.
4. Add notes or record a conversation, then tap Summarize conversation. This sends your notes and transcript to Ollama Cloud. Check the streamed result and save/reopen the conversation.
5. Try cancelling a summary and testing an invalid key. Your previous completed summary should remain available. Remove the key and confirm the saved-key indicator clears.
6. Check the purple waveform icon on your Home Screen, and test layouts on iPhone and iPad.

Apple Intelligence remains the default summary option. Microphone audio is transcribed on-device and is not saved. Recording requires a physical iPhone or iPad running iOS 26 or later. Simulator and live cloud API checks have passed; physical-device acceptance of the new cloud feature is still pending. Please review summary accuracy and send feedback through TestFlight.
