This build adds the pages the App Store requires: More → Settings → Privacy now links to the privacy policy and the support page. History shows a note when one saved conversation cannot be read instead of hiding the whole list, and that file is set aside rather than deleted; the note can be dismissed. History search now also matches your typed notes. Prompts sent to the model now mark transcript, notes and reference text as data so instructions inside them are not followed.
