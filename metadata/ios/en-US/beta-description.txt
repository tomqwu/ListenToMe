Listen To Me captures conversations with on-device microphone transcription, editable notes and local history on iPhone and iPad. Follow the transcript alongside Quick Summary, which evaluates new speech and updates when information changes. Auto can also update Summary; Deep analysis stays on Mac. Each output has its own model choice. Generate manually, ask a suggested question, or summarize selected lines. Rotate iPhone for side-by-side Transcript and Quick with adjustable pane widths. Prompt below Notes supports one-time questions or saved conversation instructions, with optional editable domain presets.

Version 1.20 adds on-device translations beneath completed transcript lines and a separate Translate summary action on the Summary page. Originals remain available. Missing language packs require explicit preparation; unsupported pairs explain recovery. Translation does not change your AI provider or replace saved originals. Check actual meaning with an installed language pair: simulator previews do not establish translation quality.

Recording checks device/language support and microphone access before enabling Start. Compatible older devices use on-device dictation when advanced transcription is unavailable. Unavailable features explain recovery. Apple Intelligence availability is checked separately; recording does not require summary AI.

Summaries use Apple Intelligence on supported devices, or a provider you explicitly select. For Ollama Cloud, enter your own API key; for local Ollama, enter your server address. You can choose an OpenAI-compatible server such as LM Studio or OpenRouter, or Cloud AI including OpenAI, Claude, Gemini, DeepSeek, OpenRouter, Groq, Mistral and xAI Grok. Keys are stored in this device's Keychain. Settings identifies the destination. Transcripts and Notes go to that provider when you request a summary or enable Auto; microphone audio stays on-device. Refresh models and test the connection in Settings.

Add local files/photos or Calendar context, or share compatible copies from Apple Notes. Original attachments stay local; only extracted text added to Notes is included in summaries. Save, reopen, delete and share conversations; export Markdown, text, subtitles or JSON; search History by meaning and ask questions with cited excerpts. Deletion clears retained answers/citations, including streaming work. Seven domain vocabularies include English and Simplified Chinese terms; My terms supports your own names and jargon. Optional Save to folder writes Markdown into a folder you choose.

The embedded Apple Watch app records and sends finished audio to its paired iPhone for on-device transcription when connected. Use Stop to finish; an audio interruption waits for Continue. Retain Watch playback audio only when Save audio recordings is on during phone import. Physical Watch recording, paired transfer/reconnection, wrist-down and battery/storage acceptance remain pending.

Requires iOS/iPadOS 26 or later and a supported speech language. Phone recording needs a physical device, works in the foreground and cannot capture other apps or phone calls. Conversation audio playback requires Save audio recordings to have been enabled. What's New can be reopened from More; an update with changed notes announces them once. Please send recording, translation, summary, import and layout feedback through TestFlight. This beta does not establish production readiness.
