ListenToMe 1.19.0 (38) — Watch beta

Test the Watch-enabled build and its paired-iPhone flows below. Hardware results remain beta feedback; simulator evidence is separate.

1. On the paired iPhone, install the iOS beta. In TestFlight app details → Information, install its compatible Apple Watch companion. Confirm the compatible Watch companion is offered for that build.
2. On Watch, inspect Record and the separate iPhone remote. Unreachable/incompatible or unready phone capture must not enable remote Start; local recording has its own microphone, battery and storage checks.
3. Record a short conversation on Watch, lower your wrist, then Stop. Check the visible indicator and elapsed time. During an interruption, verify recording waits for explicit Continue and preserves the same conversation.
4. Repeat while disconnected. Reconnect and open the iPhone app: verify pending parts arrive once, transcribe in order, and remain one conversation. Test relaunch, interrupted transfer and a recording killed mid-chunk; the known missing-part/gap wait can last up to 24 hours.
5. With Save audio recordings on during import, verify phone playback and sharing; with it off, verify transcription without retained playback audio. Imports never download speaker/language models. Test already-prepared models separately from unavailable/uncertain fallback.
6. After Stop, Delete waiting Watch audio and relaunch: deleted recordings must not return. Storage failures should preserve pending audio and end metadata for recovery. Cancellation/Stop or a readiness change during permission preparation must not start audio later. Leave during pending Start/Continue, return and retry; old replies must not change the new attempt. A temporary permission overlay must still allow granting consent. Established recording must keep running after leaving the app.
7. Check iPhone/iPad portrait, landscape Duo, Notes/Prompt, Summary, History and the Full summary menu icon.

Privacy: Watch audio moves only to its paired iPhone through WatchConnectivity. Optional summary providers retain existing explicit setup and text disclosures; audio is not sent to them. Saved playback audio remains opt-in. Pending Watch audio/end metadata may stay local until successful handoff or explicit deletion.

Simulator tests cover native controllers, real AAC files and isolated transport adapters. Physical Watch microphone/paired transfer, wrist-down, battery/storage and production acceptance remain pending; do not treat simulator or upload success as those results. This beta does not submit a new public App Store version or publish listing screenshots.
